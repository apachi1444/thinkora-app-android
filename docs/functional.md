# Functional Documentation

## ✨ Features

### 🔹 Daily Inspiration
- **Daily Quote**: Get a fresh, thought-provoking quote every day to start your morning with positivity.
- **Categorized Quotes**: Explore quotes across various categories like Life, Business, Sports, and Tech.
- **Search**: Find specific quotes or authors using the search functionality.
- **Favorites**: Save your favorite quotes to revisit them later.

### 🔹 Habit Tracking
- **Habit Management**: Create and track multiple habits.
- **Streaks**: Visualize your consistency with streak counters for each habit.
- **Home Screen Widget**: Track your habits directly from your home screen with an interactive widget.
  - **Direct Pinning**: Easily add the widget from within the app.
  - **Interactive**: Increment streaks and navigate through your habits without opening the app.

### 🔹 User Experience
- **Onboarding Flow**: A smooth onboarding experience to personalize the app (Name, Interests).
- **Notifications**: Receive daily reminders for your quotes.
- **Dark/Light Mode**: (Assumed based on standard practice, accessible via system settings).
- **Offline Support**: Access your quotes and habits even without an internet connection (via local database).
